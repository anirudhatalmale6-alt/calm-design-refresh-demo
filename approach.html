Start with PP#2
For Sandy — instead of a call

How I would run the Calm Life Skills refresh

You asked for a call to talk through the approach. I do not do calls, so here is the same conversation written down, which has the advantage that you can re-read it, forward it to your son, and hold me to it.

Everything below is based on what your site actually does today, measured rather than assumed. The numbers come from your sitemap, your page source and your Shopify store.

776addressable pages in your sitemap
~13page templates behind them
~70destinations in your current menu
5links in the supplied design's menu
4pages the supplied design covers
2014last date stamped on your sitemap
The plan

Six stages, in this order

01
In progress

Templates, before anything is touched

There is no design yet for most of the site. Rather than discover that halfway through integration, I build each missing template first, in your theme's own visual language, and you approve it on a link like the ones you already have.

  • Home page — built
  • Article detail — built, covers articles, FAQs and success stories
  • Content listing — built, one template for about 614 of the 776 URLs
  • Topic section pages (Handling Life Issues, Health, Self Improvement)
  • Seminars, Contact, Videos, Short Talks, Stress Tester, Getting Started
  • Terms and Privacy
Why first: a template approved on a static page costs minutes to change. The same change after it is woven into PHP costs hours, on a site that is live while you look at it.
02
Once access arrives

Access and a staging copy

Your son gives me FTP or SSH and a database user, plus a staging subdomain — something like staging.calm.com.au that is closed to search engines. Nothing I do touches the live site until you have clicked it on staging and said yes.

  • Read-only look at the codebase first, so I can see how the templates are actually assembled
  • A copy of the site running on the staging subdomain
  • A full backup of files and database taken before I change anything
Risk if skipped: editing a live site with 776 indexed pages means every mistake is public and is being crawled while it is wrong.
03
Once access arrives

Integration

The real work. The new stylesheet goes in, Bootstrap 3 comes out, and every PHP template is rewired to the new markup. Content, links and the back end are untouched — only the visual layer changes.

  • Remove Bootstrap 3.0.3, jQuery UI and the shims for Internet Explorer 8 that the site still loads
  • Replace the five per-page stylesheets with the one shared theme
  • Remove the iContact newsletter signup, as you asked
  • Keep every existing URL exactly as it is
The thing to watch: your menu has about 70 destinations and the supplied design has 5. Every one of the 70 stays reachable, or roughly 600 pages quietly drop out of Google.
04
Once access arrives

Cross-browser and device QA

Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge, at phone, tablet and desktop widths, with screenshots at each breakpoint so you can see it rather than take my word for it.

  • Screenshots of every template at each breakpoint
  • Every internal link checked, not sampled
  • The pages that only exist on demand checked too, not just the home page
Note: a home page that looks right proves almost nothing. The pages that break are the ones nobody thinks to open.
05
Once access arrives

Go live on calm.com.au

The design goes live on the domain you already have, with the old theme kept intact so a revert is one step, not a rebuild. Then we leave it alone for a week or two and watch that nothing moved.

  • A hand-over note listing every file touched
  • Exact revert instructions
  • The old theme kept in place, not deleted
Why not do the rename here: see the next stage.
06
Once access arrives

The move to calmlifeskills.com — separately

This is the part that can actually cost you traffic, and it has nothing to do with how the site looks. Some of your pages have been indexed since 2014. If they move address without a redirect, that history does not move with them.

  • A permanent redirect for each of the 776 addresses to its new one
  • calm.com.au stays registered and pointing at the new site indefinitely — not a year, permanently
  • Canonical tags, sitemap and Search Console change-of-address updated
  • The /article/23 to /faq/23 quirk carried across, or Google sees two copies of everything
Do not combine this with the redesign. If both land at once and traffic dips, there is no way to tell which one did it and no clean way to undo either.
The awkward questions, answered up front

Things you would have asked on the call

How do I pay you?
As we go. I do a piece, you look at it, and if you are happy I ask for a small release for that piece. I do not quote a lump sum up front and I do not ask for money for work you have not seen. The three pages you already have were free, and I said so at the time.
What happens if I do not like a template?
You say so and I change it, before it goes anywhere near your codebase. That is the entire reason the templates come first.
Can we undo this if it goes wrong?
Yes. The old theme stays on the server rather than being deleted, and the hand-over note tells you exactly which files to put back. A revert should be minutes, not a rebuild.
Will my Google rankings survive?
Through the redesign, yes — no URL changes, no content changes, and every one of the roughly 70 menu destinations stays linked. Through the domain move, only if the redirects are done properly, which is why it is its own stage.
How long?
Honestly, it depends mostly on how fast approvals and access come back, not on how fast I work. The templates I can keep producing now. Integration cannot start until there is a staging copy to do it on.

What this project does not include

  • Your Shopify shop. It lives on a different system at shop.calm.com.au and is not part of the PHP codebase. You said to leave it as is, so it keeps its current theme. The new pages link into it and those links all work, but the shop itself will not look like the new design until it is done as its own project.
  • New content. Words, articles and images stay as they are. This is a visual layer change.
  • The domain move itself, unless you ask for it. It is listed above as stage six because it needs to be planned, but it is a separate piece of work from the refresh.
  • Email on the new domain. calmlifeskills.com currently points its mail at the registrar's free forwarding. If you want real mailboxes there, that is worth sorting separately, before anything is printed with the new name on it.
Over to you

What I need to keep moving

Nothing, to carry on building templates. I can keep producing those on my own side and you approve them as they appear.

To start integration: FTP or SSH plus a database user from your son, and a staging subdomain closed to search engines.

Decisions I am waiting on: whether PP#2 is $14.95 USD or $20.00 AUD; whether the three meditation names that differ between the design and your shop should follow the design or the shop; whether the design's “How It Works” replaces Getting Started or sits beside it; and which template you want next.
