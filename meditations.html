Why this page exists

You asked for the menu to match the new design, which means five links instead of about seventy. That is the right call for a visitor — but five links cannot point at seventy places, and a page with no link pointing at it eventually drops out of Google.

So each of the five leads to a page like this one. This single page carries 68 destinations: the 19 meditation tracks and the 49 topic pages. Nothing is orphaned. It is two clicks instead of a wall of menu items, and it reads like a page rather than a directory.

The same pattern covers the rest: How It Works takes Getting Started, the six videos, the FAQs, the how-to guides and the stress tester. About Sandy takes his story, About CALM, the charity, the Vietnam tours and contact. Success Stories takes the 344 stories plus articles, videos and short talks. The footer is the second safety net.
