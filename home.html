About Sandy

Trained under fire. Now teaching calm.

Sandy MacGregor served in Vietnam, was decorated for it, and later lost three daughters to a single act of violence. What he built afterwards was not a theory about resilience. It was the thing that got him through.

Since 1989 he has taught these techniques on three continents, to boardrooms, classrooms and people in the worst week of their lives.
